About Me

From Electronics Engineering to Software Development

My Journey

My path into software wasn't straight. I started with Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering in Mumbai, but quickly realized I was spending more time coding than working with circuits. That's when I knew software was where I belonged.

The real learning happened at LTM, where I built full-stack functionality for Marsh's Policy & Placement Management platform and designed automated test suites that cut manual testing effort by 60%. Those 2 years taught me that good software isn't just about making it work — it's about making it work reliably at scale.

Working at FastFindFirm meant building web and mobile apps with React, React Native, and Node.js for a platform serving 5,000+ users across online learning and internship programs — wearing many hats and thinking about scalability from day one.

Now at Northeastern, I'm conducting research on multi-agent AI systems and human–AI interaction while diving deeper into distributed systems and machine learning. Currently maintaining a 3.92 GPA.
